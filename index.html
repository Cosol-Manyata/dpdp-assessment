Why this matters

Not a questionnaire — a compliance consultant in software

The DPDPA imposes real obligations backed by penalties of up to ₹250 crore. This platform evaluates your current controls, quantifies risk, and hands you a board-ready roadmap toward substantial compliance.

Every control maps to a real DPDPA section or DPDP Rule — no invented references.

Weighted scoring

Critical controls carry more weight, producing a defensible compliance score per domain.

Risk register

Failed controls become classified risks with likelihood, impact and mitigation.
